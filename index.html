Bonny & Bright

Bonny & Bright is an interactive personal skincare platform. It helps people build a routine around their skin, the products they already own, their budget, and product trust information.

What the app includes

  • A guided skin-profile onboarding journey.
  • An AI routine builder that considers skin needs, existing products, and budget.
  • A personal shelf to track skincare products already owned.
  • Product discovery, ingredient and sustainability trust information, shopping, subscription, store-locator, and progress-journey screens.

Important note

This public version is a front-end prototype. Visitor preferences and demo activity are stored in that visitor's browser unless the optional Supabase and Stripe production services are configured.
