Bonny & Bright

Bonny & Bright is a personal skincare platform designed to make product and routine decisions more considered. It combines skin needs, existing products, budget, and trust information rather than treating skincare as a generic product catalogue.

Core experience

  • Guided onboarding creates a skin profile based on skin type, concerns, goals, preferences, and budget.
  • The Routine Builder recommends a practical routine while considering products the person already owns.
  • My Shelf helps prevent duplicate purchases by recording existing products.
  • Shopping surfaces recommendations, budget-sensitive options, and trust information rather than a generic product grid.
  • Skin Journey, subscriptions, store locator, account settings, and an admin dashboard demonstrate the wider product experience.

Trust and commerce

The app distinguishes verified, partially verified, and unavailable product claims. The public site runs as a front-end prototype. It supports local demo activity by default; production accounts, payments, analytics, and inventory require the optional Supabase and Stripe configuration.
